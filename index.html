Sweet Bloom
The Daily Scent logo features a combination of the letters "D" and "s," representing the brand name. The curved shapes symbolize the way the fragrance lingers and spreads, while the star symbol represents distinction and confidence. As for the brand's meaning, it signifies a fragrance suitable for daily activities evoking a scent that is comfortable and perfect for sharing the moment..
